# Taker

> Taker is a Riyadh-based restaurant technology platform providing online ordering, delivery operations, and marketing tools for food & beverage businesses. Its Taker GO product exposes a delivery-integration API that connects restaurants and delivery service providers (DSPs) for order creation, tracking, and status management.

## APIs
- [Taker GO Integration API](https://api.help.taker.io/): DSP and restaurant delivery integration — create/track/cancel orders and receive status webhooks. Production base https://production.go.taker.io/, sandbox https://sandbox.go.taker.io/. Bearer-token auth.
